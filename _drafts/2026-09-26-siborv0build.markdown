### Ending notes

Fair to say, im a fan of Voron i enjoyed building this printer a lot. Someday im gonna get a 2.4 so you probably will seeing that post in the future. I'm a little disappointed with siboor but they are the cheapest voron kit you could by so some corner has to be cut. With all the issue out of the way, it's been painless I literally don't have to worry about anything when printing. 

If i have the money i would have build a trident as well but right now im lacking a large printer which 2.4 will fill the area and the trident will sit there collecting dust.

Is 0.2 a good beginner voron ? probably not, things i heard online about the frame needs to be perfectly square which can be a pain to do (i bought 123 block for it). Also you can't use drop nuts which means you **must** ensure all the nuts required are loaded. I preload some additional into the frame as i know my ass in the future is gonna add more shit to it. 

It's a fast little silly printer that can be lugged around, I love him so much actually.
